================================================================================
  STEWARDPRO – CHURCH FINANCE MODULE
  Complete Flow: Setup → Transactions → Remittance → Reporting
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
The Church Finance module manages the full financial lifecycle of an SDA local
church: chart of accounts, income collection (tithe & offerings), departmental
budgeting, expense management, fund tracking, and weekly conference remittance.

All financial documents follow Frappe's submittable-document workflow:
  DRAFT → SUBMITTED → (AMENDED or CANCELLED)
Only submitted documents affect reported balances and remittance calculations.

================================================================================
PHASE 1 – SETUP  (done once, at installation or first use)
================================================================================

STEP 1 : Church Settings
  Doctype : Church Settings  (single document)
  Path    : Church Administration → Church Settings
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Configure before any finance work begins:
    • Church Name, Church Code, Conference, Union/Mission, District
    • Fiscal Year Start Month  (e.g. January)
    • Currency                 (e.g. TZS, USD)
    • Tithe Remit Percentage   (standard SDA: 100% to conference)
    • SMS settings (optional)  – for receipt SMS on submission
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WHY: These values are referenced throughout all finance documents and
       the weekly auto-remittance scheduler.


STEP 2 : Chart of Accounts
  Doctype : Church Account  (tree / nested set)
  Path    : Church Finance → Chart of Accounts
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Root group accounts created automatically on install:
    ┌── Church Assets     (group)
    ├── Church Liabilities (group)
    ├── Church Income      (group)
    └── Church Expense     (group)

  General accounts created on install under each root:
    Church Income
      ├── Tithe Income              (subtype: Tithe)
      ├── Regular Offering Income   (subtype: Offering)
      ├── Building Fund Income      (subtype: Fund)
      └── Camp Meeting Income       (subtype: Offering)

    Church Expense
      ├── General Administration Expense  (subtype: Operating Expense)
      └── Conference Remittance Expense   (subtype: Operating Expense)

    Church Assets
      ├── Church Cash           (subtype: Cash)
      ├── Church Bank Account   (subtype: Bank)
      └── Church Property       (subtype: Property)

    Church Liabilities
      └── Accounts Payable      (subtype: Payable)

  Department accounts created automatically for every enabled department:
    Church Income
      └── <Department Name> Income   (subtype: Offering)
    Church Expense
      └── <Department Name> Expense  (subtype: Ministry Expense)
    Church Assets
      └── <Department Name> Fund     (subtype: Fund)

  Account tree balance rules:
    • Income accounts  – credits increase balance (receipts)
    • Expense accounts – debits increase balance  (spending)
    • Asset accounts   – increase on receipt, decrease when paid out
    • Group accounts   – show sum of all leaf descendants
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO ADD ACCOUNTS:
    Open tree → click a group → "Add Child" → fill Account Name,
    Account Type, Account Subtype → Save.
    The tree view shows the computed balance next to every account in
    colour: green (income/positive), orange (expense), red (negative).


STEP 3 : Church Departments
  Doctype : Church Department
  Path    : Church Administration → Church Department
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  17 default departments created on install:
    Adventist Youth, Dorcas/Community Services, Personal Ministries,
    Health Ministries, Music Ministry, Sabbath School, Deacons Ministry,
    Deaconesses Ministry, Children's Ministries, Family Ministries,
    Communication, Education, Stewardship, Women's Ministries,
    Men's Ministries, Prayer Ministries, Publishing Ministries.

  When a department is saved (and enabled = Yes), three Church Accounts
  are created automatically:
    <Dept> Income  /  <Dept> Expense  /  <Dept> Fund
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------


STEP 4 : Church Members
  Doctype : Church Member
  Path    : Church Administration → Church Member
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Members are the giving units for Tithe & Offering entries.
  Key fields: First/Last Name, Phone, Membership Status, Membership Date,
              How Joined (Baptism / Profession of Faith / Transfer In),
              Baptism details.
  On creation: welcome SMS sent automatically (if SMS enabled).
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------


STEP 5 : Church Funds  (optional)
  Doctype : Church Fund
  Path    : Church Finance → Church Fund
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Used to track special fund-raising campaigns (e.g. Building Fund drive).
  Fields: Fund Name, Fund Account (links to Church Account), Status
          (Open / Closed / Achieved), Target Amount, Current Balance,
          Start Date, Target Date.
  Current Balance is updated manually or via reports.
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------


================================================================================
PHASE 2 – BUDGETING  (annual or quarterly)
================================================================================

STEP 6 : Department Budget Request
  Doctype : Department Budget Request  (submittable)
  Path    : Church Finance → Department Budget Request
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Each department submits its expense needs for a fiscal period.

  Key fields:
    • Department       – the requesting department
    • Fiscal Year      – e.g. 2026
    • Budget Period    – Annual / Q1 / Q2 / Q3 / Q4
    • Items table      – each row: Expense Description, Account,
                         Quantity, Unit Cost → Amount (auto-calculated)
    • Total Requested Amount  (auto-summed, read-only)
    • Status: Draft → Pending Approval → Budgeted

  On Submit:
    → Status set to "Pending Approval"
    → A Church Budget document is auto-created from the request
    → church_budget field on this document is populated with the link

  On Cancel:
    → Auto-created Church Budget (if still Draft) is deleted
    → Status reverts to "Draft"
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------


STEP 7 : Church Budget
  Doctype : Church Budget  (submittable)
  Path    : Church Finance → Church Budget
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The consolidated budget for a fiscal period. Can be created directly
  or auto-generated from a Department Budget Request (Step 6).

  Key fields:
    • Budget Name, Budget Year, Budget Period
    • Start Date / End Date
    • Income Items table  – Account, Description, Budgeted Amount
    • Expense Items table – Account, Description, Budgeted Amount
    • Total Budgeted Income   (auto-summed)
    • Total Budgeted Expense  (auto-summed)
    • Surplus / Deficit       (Income – Expense, auto-calculated)
    • Approved Date / Approved By
    • Department Budget Request link (if auto-created)

  Workflow: Draft → (Church Board approves) → Submit
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------


================================================================================
PHASE 3 – INCOME COLLECTION  (weekly, each Sabbath)
================================================================================

STEP 8 : Tithe and Offering Entry
  Doctype : Tithe and Offering Entry  (submittable)
  Naming  : TOE-.YYYY.-.#####
  Path    : Church Finance → Tithe and Offering Entry
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  One record per member (or envelope) per Sabbath service.
  Can be entered by the Deacon, Treasurer, or Church Clerk.

  ENTRY TAB – Member Details:
    • Entry Date     (defaults to today)
    • Member         (optional link to Church Member)
    • Member Name    (auto-fetched from Member record)
    • Envelope Number

  ENTRY TAB – Tithe & Offerings (direct currency fields):
    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │ Field                │ Distribution                             │
    ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
    │ Tithe                │ 100% → Conference                        │
    │ Offering             │ 42% Local Church / 56% Conference        │
    │ Camp Meeting         │ 100% → Conference                        │
    │ Church Building Fund │ 100% → Local Church                      │
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  OFFERING SPLIT SECTION (auto-calculated, read-only):
    • Local Church Share %   = 42%
    • Offering – Local (42%) = Offering × 0.42
    • Conference Share %     = 56%
    • Offering – Conference  = Offering × 0.56

  TOTALS SECTION (auto-calculated, read-only):
    • Total Tithe & Offerings  = Tithe + Offering + Camp + Building Fund
    • Total for Conference     = Tithe + Offering(56%) + Camp Meeting
    • Total for Local Church   = Offering(42%) + Building Fund
    • Grand Total              = Total for Conference + Total for Local Church

  PAYMENT TAB:
    • Payment Method  – Cash / Mobile Money / Bank Transfer / Cheque
    • Reference Number (shown for non-cash payments)
    • Received By     (auto-filled with logged-in user)

  Additional Offerings (custom items):
    • Items table (Tithe Offering Item child):
        - Offering Type  (Select: Tithe / Church Budget / Sabbath School
          Offering / 13th Sabbath Offering / Investment Offering /
          Birthday & Thanksgiving / Building Fund / Evangelism Fund /
          Camp Meeting Offering / Special Offering)
        - Amount
        - Account  (manual link to Church Account)

  On Save:
    → Offering split recalculated
    → All totals recalculated
    → Negative amount validation

  On Submit:
    → Receipt SMS sent to member's phone (if SMS enabled)
    → Record becomes immutable; amend if correction needed

  ROLES allowed to create: Treasurer, Deacon, System Manager
  ROLES allowed to read:   Pastor, Church Elder (read-only)
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------


================================================================================
PHASE 4 – EXPENSE MANAGEMENT  (as needed)
================================================================================

STEP 9 : Church Expense
  Doctype : Church Expense  (submittable)
  Path    : Church Finance → Church Expense
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Records payments made by the church.

  Key fields:
    • Expense Date
    • Account       – the Expense account being debited
                      (e.g. "Adventist Youth Expense")
    • Amount
    • Paid From     – the Asset account funds are drawn from
                      (e.g. "Church Cash" or "Church Bank Account")
    • Description   (required)
    • Payment Method – Cash / Mobile Money / Bank Transfer / Cheque
    • Requested By  – Church Member who requested the expense
    • Approved By   – User who authorised payment
    • Board Minute Reference
    • Remarks

  Balance impact (reflected in tree view):
    • Account (Expense)  balance increases by Amount
    • Paid From (Asset)  balance decreases by Amount

  ROLES: Treasurer – full access; Pastor – read + submit; Others – read
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------


================================================================================
PHASE 5 – WEEKLY REMITTANCE  (every Saturday night, automated)
================================================================================

STEP 10 : Conference Remittance  (auto-created by scheduler)
  Doctype  : Conference Remittance  (submittable)
  Naming   : REM-.YYYY.-.#####
  Path     : Church Finance → Conference Remittance
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Records the amounts sent from the local church to the Conference.

  AUTO-CREATION (Saturday night scheduler):
    Trigger  : Daily scheduler checks if today is Saturday (weekday 5)
    Period   : Sunday through Saturday of the current week
    Source   : All submitted Tithe and Offering Entries in the period

    Logic:
      Total Tithe          = SUM(tithe)  from all entries in period
      Total Offering Conf  = SUM(offering_conference_amount)  (56% share)
      Total Camp Meeting   = SUM(camp_meeting)  from all entries in period

    Items created (one row per non-zero component):
      ┌────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┐
      │ Offering Type          │ Amount                  │
      ├────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┤
      │ Tithe                  │ Total Tithe             │
      │ Sabbath School Offering│ Total Offering (56%)    │
      │ Camp Meeting Offering  │ Total Camp Meeting      │
      └────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┘

    Status on creation: Draft
    Duplicate guard: if a remittance for the same period already exists
                     (non-cancelled), creation is skipped.

  MANUAL CREATION:
    Treasurer can also create manually at any time.

  Key fields:
    • Remittance Date
    • Period From / Period To
    • Status: Draft → Sent → Acknowledged
    • Items table (Remittance Item):
        - Offering Type  (Select – same 10 options as above)
        - Amount
    • Total Remittance  (auto-summed from items)
    • Payment Method    (default: Bank Transfer)
    • Reference Number
    • Receipt from Conference  (file attachment)

  Workflow:
    1. Draft created automatically each Saturday night
    2. Treasurer reviews and verifies amounts
    3. Payment sent to Conference → status set to "Sent"
    4. Conference confirms receipt → file attached → status "Acknowledged"
    5. Treasurer submits the document to lock it

  ROLES: Treasurer – full access; Pastor – read + write + submit
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------


================================================================================
PHASE 6 – ACCOUNT BALANCE VIEW
================================================================================

STEP 11 : Chart of Accounts Tree View
  Path : Church Finance → Chart of Accounts
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The tree view shows every account with its computed balance in real time.

  Balance sources:
    Income accounts
      • "Tithe Income"            ← SUM(tithe) from submitted entries
      • "Regular Offering Income" ← SUM(offering_local_amount) from entries
      • "Building Fund Income"    ← SUM(church_building_fund) from entries
      • "Camp Meeting Income"     ← SUM(camp_meeting) from entries
      • Department income accts   ← SUM(tithe_offering_item.amount) where
                                     account = this account

    Expense accounts
      • SUM(church_expense.amount) where account = this account

    Asset accounts
      • Reduced by SUM(church_expense.amount) where paid_from = this acct

    Group accounts
      • SUM of all leaf-account balances within their lft/rgt bounds

  Colour coding:
    Green  – Income / positive asset balance
    Orange – Expense (spending shown positive)
    Red    – Negative balance (overdraft)

  Toolbar (Treasurer / System Manager only):
    "Refresh Balances" button – recomputes and persists balance on every
    account record in one pass.
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------

KEY CALCULATED FIELDS  (all auto-computed, read-only)
================================================================================

  Tithe and Offering Entry:
    offering_local_amount      = offering × 42%
    offering_conference_amount = offering × 56%
    total_tithe_and_offerings  = tithe + offering + camp_meeting + building_fund
    total_for_conference       = tithe + offering_conference_amount + camp_meeting
    total_for_local_church     = offering_local_amount + building_fund
    grand_total                = total_for_conference + total_for_local_church

  Church Budget:
    total_budgeted_income  = SUM(income_items.budgeted_amount)
    total_budgeted_expense = SUM(expense_items.budgeted_amount)
    surplus_deficit        = total_budgeted_income – total_budgeted_expense

  Department Budget Request:
    total_requested_amount = SUM(items.amount)
    item.amount            = quantity × unit_cost  (if both provided)

  Conference Remittance:
    total_remittance = SUM(items.amount)
